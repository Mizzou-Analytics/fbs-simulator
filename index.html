Filters the teams, what-ifs, results and charts below. The simulation always covers all of FBS.

Game model

Ratings are points better than an average FBS team. "Results model" rebuilds ratings from this season's scores, starting from the preseason ratings with the prior weight you set (in games) and capping blowout margins. Prior weight and margin cap only affect the results model, and they also fill in any team the chosen source doesn't rate. The same seed gives the same results.

Playoff model

In every simulated season, each FBS team gets a committee score: rating − loss penalty × losses + champion bonus (conference champions) + SoS weight × average opponent rating + noise. The highest-scoring conference champions get the automatic bids, and the rest of the field goes to the highest remaining scores. Seeding follows the score order, and the top seeds get byes. The bracket is then played out, with first-round games at the higher seed and later rounds at neutral sites.
