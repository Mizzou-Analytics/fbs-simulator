National playoff picture

The projected field: the teams most likely to make the playoff, seeded by average finish (missing the field counts as one spot below the last seed). Each line gives the projected record and the chance of a bye, of hosting a first-round game, or of making the field; hover for the chance of that exact seed. Teams from the selected conference are in red.

Playoff odds

Every team with at least a 0.5% chance, most likely first. SOR is strength of record: projected wins above what a bubble team would win against the same schedule. The seed columns shade from blank to solid ink as a seed becomes more likely (a dot is under 0.5%); the round columns give the chance of reaching each round.

What-if

Force a winner for any remaining game. Percentages are the home team's pregame win probability ("vs" = neutral site). What-ifs in other weeks and conferences stay in effect.

Teams

Records come from final scores. Once the rating source has two or more weeks of history, a sparkline traces it week by week, ending at the red dot. Δ wk is the change since last week. Type an override to replace a team's rating; clear it to go back to the source.

Settings

Changes apply on the next run.

Game model

Ratings are points better than an average FBS team. "Results model" rebuilds ratings from this season's scores, starting from the preseason ratings with the prior weight you set (in games) and capping blowout margins. Prior weight and margin cap only affect the results model, and they also fill in any team the chosen source doesn't rate. The same seed gives the same results.
