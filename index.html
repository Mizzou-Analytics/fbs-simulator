How this works

Each day, schedules, scores and team ratings are pulled from CollegeFootballData.com: SP+ by default, with FPI, Elo and SRS available. Every remaining game is then simulated 10,000 times. A team's strength is a rating in points, with uncertainty about the rating itself plus game-to-game randomness and home-field advantage.

In each simulated season, conference standings are settled with each conference's tiebreakers, title games are played, and a simulated committee picks and seeds the field. It ranks teams on rating and strength of record (wins compared with what a bubble team would manage against the same schedule). Conference champions get a bonus, and head-to-head results settle close calls. The five highest-ranked conference champions get automatic bids.

The settings were tuned by replaying the 2024 and 2025 seasons from week 6, week 10 and selection day, with no peeking at later results. The tuning improved the forecasts of the playoff field and byes in every case tested. Two seasons is a small sample, so treat these numbers as informed estimates, not certainties.

Adjust the model

Games

Ratings are points better than an average FBS team. "Results model" rebuilds ratings from this season's scores, starting from the preseason ratings with the prior weight you set (in games) and capping blowout margins. The same seed gives the same results.

Save or load settings

Export saves your settings, rating overrides and picks to a file. Import loads one of those files, or a full season data file. Reset returns everything to the defaults.
